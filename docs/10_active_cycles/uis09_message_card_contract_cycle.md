# UIS09 — Message Card Contract Cycle

Status: completed
Date: 2026-07-19
Cycle code: UIS09

## Objective

Recognize Proposal, Inbox and AI Chat history as one message-card family without erasing their domain differences.

## Delivered

- Declared `message-card` with main, title, aside, summary, attachment, footer and action elements.
- Applied it to Proposal, Inbox and AI Chat list cards.
- Preserved proposal/inbox/chat classes for status, content and bounded variants.

## Acceptance

New message-like surfaces can start from the neutral anatomy instead of copying Proposal markup conventions.
