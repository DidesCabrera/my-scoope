# 0176 Consumer-first mobile product direction

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-08-05

## Context

Earlier App Store planning treated nutritionists and invited members as principal
mobile customers. The intended initial market is instead people who personally
weigh food, follow a structured program and pursue physical change.

## Decision

My Scoope's first mobile product is B2C. Fat loss, muscle gain and body
recomposition are its initial goal categories. Nutritionist workspaces, invited
clients, seat purchasing and professional mobile flows are deferred.

The MVP optimizes the daily loop of planning, following, measuring and reviewing
one's own program. App Store architecture and acceptance criteria must not add
nutritionist-specific complexity to this release.

## Consequences

- The mobile API is vertical and consumer-scoped rather than web-parity scoped.
- B2C digital subscriptions require an in-app Apple purchase path on iOS.
- Product success is measured through activation, adherence, progress use,
  retention and conversion rather than professional-seat adoption.
- Future nutritionist work starts with its own evidence and cycle.
