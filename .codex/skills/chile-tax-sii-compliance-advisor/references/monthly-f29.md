# Monthly F29 preparation for My Scoope

Use this procedure when Felipe asks to prepare, review, or complete a monthly F29. It describes the handoffs and evidence required for a repeatable close; it does not establish a tax position. Confirm the entity, period, regime, activities, obligations, and filing status in live SII records before calculating.

## Sources and working location

- Read the dated fiscal evidence index linked from `.codex/team/memory/areas/finance.md`. The live SII account and original documents control over the index or a prior chat.
- The operating register is [My Scoope - Registro Financiero](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/19y3l6Ywv7zJ90Zvai_WXMIlWGf9t3D6U2tH9y9KPjXU/edit); verify its current tabs and rows. The local `FINANZAS/FACTURAS` folder contains working and historical evidence. Its files are not proof of durable archival in Drive. The approved Drive location and actual period folder must be confirmed from the current finance process; do not create or assume an unapproved canonical path.
- Use this folder contract when Felipe establishes the monthly workspace: `FINANZAS/FACTURAS/<YYYY_MM_Mes>/<Proveedor>/` for original supplier documents and purchase DTE, following the existing local naming pattern; `FINANZAS/F29/<YYYY-MM>/` for `01_inventario`, `02_SII_RCV`, `03_calculo`, `04_revision`, and `05_presentacion_pago`. Put a manifest in the F29 period folder linking each item to the original, rather than duplicating invoices. This F29 folder is a proposed structure until Felipe confirms its actual local or Drive location; do not claim it exists, create it, or reorganize existing files without authorization. If he supplies a different approved folder, map the same evidence categories there and record the path for future cycles.
- At handoff, identify the actual period folder or manifest and check that it links to provider invoices, purchase DTE, payment or card-charge evidence, SII exports, the draft calculation, and later the filed return and payment receipt. Record the period and cutoff timestamp, manifest version or link, document status (original, archive, register, payment), totals by currency, and open duplicates or exceptions. If new documents arrive after cutoff, add them to a new version and recompute affected amounts. Keep source documents and sensitive values in the approved finance repository, not in repository team memory.
- For each document record supplier, document identity and folio, issue date, charge or payment date, currency, net/tax/total, Chilean DTE type and folio if any, source link, SII status, and unresolved exception. Preserve the original; do not infer a missing value from a receipt or a spreadsheet label.

## Handoff and stages

1. **Felipe gathers evidence.** Felipe may quickly collect outstanding documents into the agreed period folder. The tax role gives him a short, prioritized missing-document list, with a reason and source for each request. If document retrieval, durable archiving, bookkeeping registration, or bank matching is delegated, route it to `accounting-intake-recordkeeping-agent` under its own authority. A folder being populated does not mean the period is ready to calculate.
2. **Tax role inventories and checks completeness.** Compare the handed-off manifest, folder, operating register, provider billing history where available, and SII DTE/Registro de Compras y Ventas (RCV). Detect possible duplicates using supplier, document number or DTE folio, date, amount, and source identity; mark their draft treatment explicitly and return the exception to `accounting-intake-recordkeeping-agent` for confirmation and any authorized correction to the canonical archive or register. Keep distinct states for original found, payment evidenced, bank matched, DTE issued, RCV reflected, and tax treatment reviewed. Return a document-level exception list; do not silently omit uncertain items.
3. **Tax role establishes treatment and period.** For each operation, distinguish the foreign invoice date, service period, charge or payment date, Chilean DTE issue date, and proposed F29 period. Verify whether the supplier charged Chilean VAT, whether a factura de compra and IVA retention apply, and whether another obligation such as F50 needs separate review. For foreign services, the [SII guidance on purchase-invoice timing](https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/iva_serv_digitales/001_200_7776.htm) ties issue to the month of the charge or payment; do not assume an invoice issued later resolves earlier-period treatment. Flag prior-period differences as a separate review queue, without treating the current ordinary F29 as a rectification.
4. **Tax role calculates a draft by code.** Reconcile sales and purchases in RCV, DTE, credit/debit notes, prior F29 balance, withholding, PPM, and other active obligations. For each F29 code show the source documents, arithmetic, proposed amount, SII proposed amount, difference, and reviewer decision. Calculate IVA retained on purchase invoices separately from possible input VAT credit. Retention payable is not silently offset by a possible credit. For foreign-service purchase invoices with total retention, verify the current SII instruction for [manual F29 code 39](https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/iva_serv_digitales/001_200_7779.htm); confirm the document's actual regime before using the code. The [SII proposal guide](https://www.sii.cl/destacados/f29/incluyepropuesta.htm) says purchase invoices under change of VAT subject require completion before submission.
   - When purchase invoices appear in RCV as recoverable but their credit is absent from the F29 proposal, reconcile each folio and amount before deciding whether to complete the credit fields. The [F29 instructions for codes 519 and 520](https://www.sii.cl/documentos/resoluciones/2011/reso42_anexo3.pdf) include purchase invoices issued by an agent retenedor when they give a right to credit. RCV classification alone does not establish that right; check the applicable service, use, document validity, and current SII treatment. Do not turn one period's accepted amounts into a standing rule.
   - The [SII's interpretation of IVA payment postponement](https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/jurisprudencia_administrativa/ley_impuesto_ventas/2018/ja962.htm) excludes tax retained from third parties. If the only payable IVA is a code 39 retention, do not present code 756 as a way to defer that payment. Recheck the current rule and the taxpayer's other IVA before advising on postponement.
5. **Felipe and the designated professional review.** Present the calculation, reconciliation, source links, amount and due date, unresolved differences, and proposed treatment. Obtain professional validation when treatment, credit entitlement, an earlier-period issue, or a material discrepancy remains uncertain. Do not represent a draft as approved because the arithmetic balances. Resolve blockers or record why a bounded decision can proceed.
6. **Submission and payment.** Before submission verify the taxpayer identity, period, form, regime, prior filing status for that period, and approved amount. Obtain Felipe's specific authorization to present that F29; authorization to pay is a separate decision. Use only permitted SII access. Felipe handles any required personal authentication, signature, or payment action. After presentation, compare the SII folio and filed form with the approved draft. After any separately authorized payment, compare its confirmation with the filed liability. Record rejection, observation, or a filed-but-unpaid state explicitly.
7. **Close and improve.** Reconcile the filed amounts and payment with the draft, DTE, RCV, and operating register. Track evidence gathered, accounting register reconciled, tax draft, reviewed, authorized to present, presented, authorized to pay, paid, and closed as separate states. Update the period's exception queue and closure status in the approved finance records when authorized. Record a candidate procedural learning with SII source URL, effective or update date, date checked, affected periods, and open questions. Promote it to a skill rule only after checking a current official source and obtaining professional validation where the treatment requires judgment. Link to sensitive evidence rather than copying it into skill or team memory.
   - Match the SII declaration certificate to the approved period and final F29 amounts; match the payment receipt to the same period and amount. A declaration certificate proves presentation, while a successful bank receipt evidences payment. Record both identifiers and evidence locations separately, without copying bank account details into the skill or team memory. Do not mark the general accounting month closed merely because its F29 was presented and paid.

## Current-source check for every period

Before relying on a deadline, code, exemption, credit, or filing method, consult the live [SII tax calendar](https://misiir.sii.cl/cgi_calendario/calendario.cgi), [F29 and proposal guidance](https://www.sii.cl/destacados/f29/), [Operación IVA / RCV guidance](https://www.sii.cl/destacados/operacion_iva/), and the applicable SII circular, resolution, FAQ, or law for the disputed treatment. Record the page, publication or update date when shown, access date, and conclusion in the period workpaper. If sources conflict or an FAQ is only general guidance, use the applicable norm and escalate the specific case for professional review. Do not carry forward last month's deadline or a prefilled SII amount without checking.

## Completion gate

The draft is ready for Felipe's decision only when the in-scope document inventory is reconciled or every remaining exception is explicit, each material F29 amount traces to evidence, retention and credit are separated, the current SII proposal has been compared, and the deadline and payment route have been verified. Explicitly check apparent no-movement periods, documents received after cutoff, foreign suppliers, missing or late DTE, RCV differences, prior-period corrections, and filings without payment. If a required source is unavailable, report the smallest missing-evidence list and the calculation's limits instead of a definitive payable amount.
