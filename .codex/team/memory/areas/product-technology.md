## Objective

Advance My Scoope's consumer outcome while preserving reliability, architectural
coherence, and a fast path from observed problem to verified correction.

## Roles

- `strategic-product-advisor`
- `ai-architecture-context-steward`
- `observability-lead`
- incident specialists for mobile, backend, and release boundaries
- `incident-verifier`

## Durable boundaries

Product behavior and architecture live in the applicable code, tests, migrations,
decisions, and `docs/00_current/`. This file records team context only. Incident
coordination begins with `observability-lead`; a direct specialist conversation is
also valid when Felipe already knows the affected boundary.

## Open evaluation

The four incident specialist skills are pilots. Promote them after repeated cases
show distinct evidence sources, decisions, and validation responsibilities.
