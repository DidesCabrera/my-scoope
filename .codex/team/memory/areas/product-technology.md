# Product and Technology Memory

Status: active
Last reviewed: 2026-11-14

## Objective

Advance My Scoope's consumer outcome while preserving reliability, architectural
coherence, and a fast path from observed problem to verified correction.

## Roles

- `strategic-product-advisor`
- `nutrition-solver-steward`
- `ai-architecture-context-steward`
- `ai-application-platform-steward`
- `mobile-store-release-steward`
- `observability-lead`
- incident specialists for mobile, backend, and release boundaries
- `incident-verifier`

## Durable boundaries

Product behavior and architecture live in the applicable code, tests, migrations,
decisions, and `docs/00_current/`. This file records team context only. Incident
coordination begins with `observability-lead`; a direct specialist conversation is
also valid when Felipe already knows the affected boundary.

## Open evaluation

The four incident specialist skills are pilots. Promote them after repeated cases
show distinct evidence sources, decisions, and validation responsibilities.

`nutrition-solver-steward` entered pilot on 2026-11-11. It owns the solver quality
model, catalog-consumption contract, evaluation scenarios, failure classification,
and improvement backlog. Product retains outcome and investment decisions;
Catalog Operations retains source, curation, and publication authority; accepted
architecture and implementation remain authoritative in code and current docs.

`ai-application-platform-steward` entered pilot on 2026-11-11. It owns the applied
AI platform lifecycle: assistant orchestration, model/provider integration,
governed capabilities and actions, MCP projection, evaluation environments,
quality gates, observability, and controlled rollout. The current executable
baseline is the AI Assistant capability catalog and Evaluation Lab v2; this team
memory does not replace their current feature contracts or implementation.

`mobile-store-release-steward` entered pilot on 2026-11-14. Its first evaluation
is the product checklist for the first public iOS App Review submission. It must
recover and reuse prior privacy, legal, metadata, and account evidence when still
valid; it may request revalidation only after naming the prior evidence and the
change, expiry, incident, rejection, or policy delta that invalidated it.
