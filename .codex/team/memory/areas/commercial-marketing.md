# Commercial and Marketing Memory

Status: active
Last reviewed: 2026-10-23

## Objective

Build evidence about customers, positioning, channels, acquisition, retention, and
revenue without converting early hypotheses into commitments or automated outreach.

## Roles

- `commercial-strategy-advisor`
- `customer-market-researcher`
- `growth-marketing-operator`
- `revenue-operations-agent`

## Current posture

All four roles are pilots. Early work should prioritize customer evidence and small
commercial experiments. Campaign automation, advertising spend, and CRM mutation
require an explicitly authorized system and workflow.

## Configuration gaps

- target customer hypotheses and research consent process;
- approved analytics sources and definitions;
- messaging and brand constraints;
- commercial pipeline or CRM source of truth;
- channel budgets, publishing permissions, and contact policies.
