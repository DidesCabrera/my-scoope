# Decision 0001: Role-based AI team

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-23
Decision owner: Felipe Dides

## Decision

My Scoope will develop a role-based AI team organized around product and technology,
finance, and commercial and marketing capabilities. Felipe can work directly with
every role. Coordinators may delegate bounded work to specialists when the problem
crosses distinct evidence or validation boundaries.

An AI Team Capability Steward will advise Felipe on team design and maintain the
skill portfolio as the company grows. It will favor a small, sufficient set of
roles and will use real work as evidence for activation, revision, merging, or
retirement.

## Consequences

- Role definitions and organizational metadata are versioned in the repository.
- New specialist roles begin as pilots unless already proven.
- Creating a role grants no new authority over external systems.
- Team memory helps future conversations but does not replace product or business
  sources of truth.
