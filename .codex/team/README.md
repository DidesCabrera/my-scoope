# My Scoope AI Team

Status: active
Owner: Felipe Dides

This directory is the organizational home for My Scoope's AI team. It answers:

- which roles exist and why;
- how work is routed and delegated;
- which roles are active, provisional, or under evaluation;
- where durable context for an area or initiative is recorded.

Use the files in this order:

1. `organization.md` for the operating model and organigram;
2. `capability-registry.yaml` for the machine-readable role and shared-skill inventory;
3. the selected `.codex/skills/<name>/SKILL.md` for behavior and limits;
4. `memory/README.md` and the relevant area or project memory for durable context.

## Authority boundary

This directory is authoritative for AI-team organization only. It does not define
product behavior, architecture, data contracts, permissions, tax treatment,
accounting policy, pricing commitments, or accepted company decisions. Those
claims must be verified in their applicable authoritative source.

The Knowledge Center is outside this system and must not be used as evidence for
AI-team or product behavior.

## Operating principle

Keep the team small enough to understand and broad enough to cover recurring
work. Create or split a role when its responsibility, evidence, tools, authority,
and validation differ materially from existing roles. Collaboration is temporary
and task-driven; roles do not convene merely because they share an area.

Use a shared workflow skill when several roles need the same repeatable procedure
but responsibility and authority remain with the role performing the work. Shared
skills do not occupy a position in the organigram and grant no additional authority.

Felipe retains final authority over role creation, activation, retirement, material
business decisions, and consequential external actions.
